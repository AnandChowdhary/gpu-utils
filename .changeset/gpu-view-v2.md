gpu-view v2: moved onto the shared scan model family (canonical WGSL kernel, no package kernel), a `primary` flag on schema fields for date disambiguation, and wider coverage: comparative and superlative adjectives that resolve through field aliases (`cheapest first`, `taller than 50 cm`), unit suffixes on numbers, full dates and seasons (`september 10 2026`, `last winter`), year-like numeric fields, `top N` stranded before its sort field, and enum values that override a carrier noun. Adds a second hand-written unfamiliar evaluation set and keeps reporting the first.
